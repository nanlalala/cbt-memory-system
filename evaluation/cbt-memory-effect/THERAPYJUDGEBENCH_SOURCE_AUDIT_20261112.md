human expert scores. Acquiring these materials does not evaluate this project's
memory system or create a new clinician review of its answers.

## Actual evidence and boundaries

- 116 **simulated** LLM patient/therapist conversations, ten ordered messages
  each (five exchanges), eight therapist model configurations and 18 fictional
  patient profiles. These are not professional therapists' utterances or actual
  patients' histories, and are not longitudinal multi-visit records.
- The publication states that **two licensed CBT-trained practitioners** rated
  the conversations. Twenty percent were independently double-annotated; the
  remainder were singly annotated. Public labels are consolidated, so do not
  infer two independent labels per row or calculate unavailable inter-rater
  reliability from the consolidated file.
- Eleven CTRS dimensions, scores 0–6: **993 nonmissing aspect ratings**. Report
  each dimension's own nonmissing denominator. Null remains null, never zero.
- Four safety categories have 111 available records and five all-null records
  (Conversations 28, 33, 52, 53, 60). Positive support is respectively **0, 26,
  4, 49** for medical advice, crisis, abuse and functional impairment. With no
  medical-advice positives, sensitivity in that category is not estimable.
- These published human labels can anchor assessor calibration. They are not
  independent human ratings of new answers generated by this project. Do not
  present judge agreement as a measured gain in memory or clinical outcomes.

## Leakage and version controls

The webpage preview exposed Conversation 1–15 and associated labels during
source discovery. Mark these **development-exposed**, and propagate exposure to
all records sharing their `patient_profile`. Split by profile rather than row.
Unexposed profiles are merely candidates; no sealed test split is claimed.
The collector writes the actual complete profile/exposure mapping after loading
the pinned source. All original files, clinical labels, schema audit and mapping
remain under `assessor_only/`; no generation inputs are exported. A later judge
protocol must blind expert labels and avoid tuning against its holdout.

Do not mix the earlier anonymous `neurips-ed-2026-sub3717` release or its five
safety fields with canonical v2's four revised safety categories. The published
file hash and exact schema are mandatory, even if an older paper abstract gives
a different annotation count. Preserve source scores without recomputation.
